# Centari — Vision & Mission

## Mission

Build tools that make complex physical and digital problems tractable — through spatial interfaces, embedded intelligence, and purposeful hardware design.

## Vision

A world where XR and AI lower the barrier to understanding and acting on real-world problems. Not spectacle — utility.

## Core principles

**Grounded in the physical.** Our products interact with the real world through sensors, actuators, and spatial context. Software abstractions serve physical outcomes.

**Intelligence at the edge.** AI capabilities belong close to where decisions are made — in the device, in the space, not only in the cloud.

**Small surface, deep value.** We build fewer things, built well. A product that solves one hard problem precisely beats a platform that solves many problems poorly.

**Open process.** We document decisions, sprint goals, and architectural tradeoffs in this repository. The process is part of the product.

## Focus areas

| Domain | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **XR interfaces** | Spatial computing tools for industrial, creative, and professional contexts |
| **Embedded AI** | On-device inference pipelines for sensors and actuators |
| **Hardware prototyping** | Custom electronics and mechanical systems bridging digital and physical |

## What we are not building

- Consumer entertainment experiences with no practical application
- Cloud-only AI wrappers with no hardware integration
- General-purpose platforms competing with established incumbents
